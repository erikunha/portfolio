<h2 id="rules">Rules</h2>

- Reference colors through Tailwind utilities backed by `@theme` (`text-primary-500`, `border-primary-subtle`). Never write raw hex in components.
- Do not use the signal green for paragraph text. Body copy uses `text` (`tertiary-50`).
- Text and surface pairs are checked by the contrast gate in CI.
