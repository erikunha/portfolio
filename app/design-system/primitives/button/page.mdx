Small and medium buttons have a 44px minimum height. Large buttons have a 52px minimum height.

<h2 id="disabled">Disabled</h2>

<Preview id="button-disabled">
  <Button disabled>DISABLED</Button>
</Preview>

An anchor cannot be natively disabled. With `as="a"` and `disabled`, the button drops `href`, sets `aria-disabled="true"`, leaves the tab order and cancels click. That path attaches a click handler, so it is used from client components only.

<h2 id="accessibility">Accessibility</h2>

Every size meets the 44px target. Keyboard focus shows a 2px signal outline with a 2px offset through `:focus-visible`. This component defaults `type` to `"button"`, opting out of the browser's implicit submit inside a `<form>`.
